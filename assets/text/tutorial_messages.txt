# The tutorial's words: what Xenocorp says (msg.*), the objectives listed on the HUD (obj.*),
# and what the results say once an exercise is passed (explain.*) or failed (fail.*).
#
# One entry a line: key = text. Lines starting with # are notes and are ignored.
# Words in {braces} are filled in by the game: {hemisphere}, {sunside}, {coastline},
# {coastdistance}, {temperature}, {tolerance}, {altitude}, {plant}, {animal}, {rainfall},
# {landmark}. Keep the ones an entry already has (a message that's spoken can't say them,
# so they're only in obj.*, explain.* and fail.* entries).
#
# After changing a msg.* entry, re-record it in the Xenocorp voice:
#     python tools/record_tutorial.py
# (only the changed ones are recorded). Until then the new words scroll along the bottom
# without the voice.

# ------------------------------------------------------------------ the landing pod (said while the planet loads)

msg.pod = Training deployment in progress. You are being sent to Xenocorp's designated training planet, which has been chosen for its safety, its convenience, and its great distance from our lawyers. Please remain seated until the hatch opens. Please do not touch anything. Especially that.

# ------------------------------------------------------------------ 1. the sun

msg.sun.welcome = Welcome to the Xenocorp Training Programme. You have been selected for training on the basis of having applied. Over the following exercises you will learn to work out where you are by using only your standard-issue equipment. Your progress is being monitored. Your mistakes are being recorded. Several already have been observed.
msg.sun.walk = A marker has been placed in the distance ahead of you. Walk to it using W, A, S and D. Should you find walking tedious, hold Shift to sprint. Xenocorp does not pay overtime to slow walkers.
msg.sun.spin = This circle marks the spot. Stand in it and turn a full circle using the mouse. You may not leave the circle until you have finished. This is to confirm that you can turn on the spot, which, regrettably, can no longer be assumed.
msg.sun.spun.clockwise = Thank you. Most trainees, when asked to turn on the spot, turn anticlockwise. You, on the other hand, turned clockwise. Our psychologists describe this as a bit strange. It has been noted on your file.
msg.sun.spun.anticlockwise = Thank you. Most trainees, when asked to turn on the spot, turn clockwise. You, on the other hand, turned anticlockwise. Our psychologists describe this as a bit strange. It has been noted on your file.
msg.sun.jump = Now press Space to jump. Jumping is useful for getting over rocks, fences and guard rails. It is also recommended for those moments when you find your work too enjoyable and need some way to express it.
msg.sun.compass = You have been issued a compass. Press 1 to hold it up, then turn until you are facing the sun. When the sun is in the north of the sky, you are in the planet's southern hemisphere. When it is in the south of the sky, you are in the northern hemisphere. When it is hidden by cloud, you are, in the technical sense, fu**ed. Today's sky has been cleared for you at considerable expense. Don't forget, the red needle points north, some trainees needed this to be explained not to name any names … Frank.
msg.sun.map = Good. Now press Tab to expand your map. Scroll to zoom in and out, and click to place a marker where you think you are, then confirm your guess. For this exercise, any guess in the correct hemisphere will be accepted.

obj.sun.walk = Walk to the marker (W, A, S, D; Shift to sprint)
obj.sun.spin = Turn a full circle inside the circle
obj.sun.jump = Jump (Space)
obj.sun.compass = Face the sun with your compass (1)
obj.sun.expand = Expand the map (Tab)
obj.sun.guess = Guess in the right hemisphere

explain.sun = The sun was in the {sunside} of the sky, so you were in the {hemisphere} hemisphere: anywhere in it would do.
fail.sun = That's the wrong hemisphere. The sun was in the {sunside}: think again about which half of the planet that puts you in.

# ------------------------------------------------------------------ 2. the coast

msg.coast.intro = Exercise two. You have landed near the coast. Coastlines are long, wiggly and, for our purposes, extremely useful. Walk to the marker on the shore.
msg.coast.compass = Now hold up your compass and look along the coastline, so that the needle tells you which way it runs. Then find a stretch of coast on your map that runs the same way and place your marker on the map.

obj.coast.walk = Walk to the marker on the shore
obj.coast.line = Look along the coast with your compass (1)
obj.coast.guess = Guess on a coast running the same way

explain.coast = The coast here runs {coastline}, and the pod came down {coastdistance} from the sea: anywhere about as near a coast running that way would do.
fail.coast = The coast there doesn't run {coastline}, or you guessed too far from it. Line the coast up with your compass again, and note which side the sea is on.

# ------------------------------------------------------------------ 3. temperature

msg.temperature.intro = Exercise three. Your compass has been withdrawn, so that you do not come to depend on it. You have been issued a thermometer. Although the red fluid in the thermometer may look tasty, we advise that you do not attempt to drink it. It is, surprisingly, highly toxic. Press 1 to hold it up and wait for the reading to settle. Then open your map, show the temperature heatmap, and guess somewhere exactly as warm as here.
msg.temperature.read = Good. The temperature heatmap shows the planet's average temperature, although this does change from day to day and season to season. Despite multiple complaints, no attempts have been made to rectify this inconvenience something about locking the entire planet in permanent day upsets the locals. Anyway, find the colour on the temperature heatmap that matches your reading.

obj.temperature.read = Take a temperature reading (1)
obj.temperature.map = Show the temperature heatmap
obj.temperature.guess = Guess somewhere as warm as here

explain.temperature = It was {temperature} here on an average day, so anywhere the heatmap reads within {tolerance} of that would do.
fail.temperature = The heatmap there reads {guessed}, and your thermometer read {temperature}. Find somewhere the colours match.

# ------------------------------------------------------------------ 4. altitude

msg.altitude.intro = Exercise four. Your thermometer has been withdrawn. You have been issued an altimeter. Press 1 to hold it up and wait for the needle to settle. Then show the contours on your map and guess somewhere at the same height. Each contour line is ten metres apart.

obj.altitude.read = Take an altitude reading (1)
obj.altitude.map = Show the contours on the map
obj.altitude.guess = Guess somewhere at the same height

explain.altitude = You were {altitude} above the sea, right on a contour line: anywhere along it would do.
fail.altitude = The ground there is {guessed} above the sea, and your altimeter read {altitude}. Follow the contour line instead.

# ------------------------------------------------------------------ 5. plants and animals

msg.wildlife.intro = Exercise five. You have been issued an identification kit. Press 1 to hold up the magnifying glass, and look at the local wildlife through it until each one is named. First, identify five of the plant named in your objectives. Other plants do not count, however much you like them. Should the wildlife be up a slope, hold Space to help you climb. Some slopes are too steep to climb at all, and you are reminded that broken legs from falling down mountains are not covered by your Xenocorp health insurance.
msg.wildlife.animals = Thank you. Now identify five of the animal named in your objectives. Do not attempt to catch it and pet it. They do not like it, and bites are not covered.
msg.wildlife.maps = Well done. You have been given maps of where these two species live. Show each of them on your map, and find where both of them are plentiful.

obj.wildlife.plants = Identify the {plant} ({count} of 5)
obj.wildlife.animals = Identify the {animal} ({count} of 5)
obj.wildlife.plantmap = Show where the {plant} grows
obj.wildlife.animalmap = Show where the {animal} lives
obj.wildlife.guess = Guess where both are plentiful

explain.wildlife = Both the {plant} and the {animal} are plentiful only where the map is green: anywhere there would do.
fail.wildlife = The {plant} and the {animal} aren't both plentiful there. Show both maps and look for where they overlap.

# ------------------------------------------------------------------ 6. rain

msg.rainfall.intro = Exercise six. Rain. Wet places have more cloud, more rain, more rivers, and more of everything that grows. Dry places have less of everything, including hope. You have been given the rainfall map and the rivers. It is very wet here. Guess somewhere just as wet. As nobody can measure rain by looking at it, anywhere in the wetter half of the planet will do.

obj.rainfall.map = Show the rainfall map
obj.rainfall.rivers = Show the rivers
obj.rainfall.guess = Guess somewhere as wet as here

explain.rainfall = It's very wet here, about {rainfall} of rain a year: anywhere in the wetter half of the planet would do.
fail.rainfall = That's in the drier half of the planet. Look at the rainfall map for the wetter places.

# ------------------------------------------------------------------ 7. the ground

msg.ground.intro = Exercise seven. The ground. The colour of the soil, and how many rocks lie on it and how big they are, change from place to place. You have been given the soil colour map and the rock maps. Look down. Yes, at the ground. Look closely at both, use every one of the maps, then guess somewhere the ground is the same.

obj.ground.soil = Show the soil colour map
obj.ground.rocks = Show the rock frequency map
obj.ground.size = Show the rock size map
obj.ground.guess = Guess somewhere the ground matches

explain.ground = Places with the same soil colour, as many rocks and rocks as small as here would do.
fail.ground = The soil, the number of rocks or their size there doesn't match here. Compare all three maps with the ground at your feet.

# ------------------------------------------------------------------ 8. grass

msg.grass.intro = Exercise eight. Grass. It is thicker, taller, greener or browner from one place to the next. You have been given the grass maps. Compare the grass at your feet with them. This is, genuinely, part of your job.

obj.grass.colour = Show the grass colour map
obj.grass.height = Show the grass height map
obj.grass.guess = Guess somewhere the grass matches

explain.grass = Places where the grass is the same colour and height as here would do.
fail.grass = The grass there isn't the same colour or height as here. Compare it again.

# ------------------------------------------------------------------ 9. wealth

msg.wealth.intro = Exercise nine. Money. Some places are richer than others, and this is not one of them. Damaged and dirty houses are a sure sign of poverty; so are poor roads and few shops. You have been given the wealth map, with the roads, buildings and shops. Look at the houses around you, judge how badly off this place is, and guess somewhere just as poor. You are reminded that your own salary is not much better.

obj.wealth.map = Show the wealth map
obj.wealth.buildings = Show the buildings
obj.wealth.guess = Guess somewhere as well off as here

explain.wealth = Places about as poor as here would do: look for damaged, dirty houses.
fail.wealth = That place is richer or poorer than here. Look at the state of the houses again.

# ------------------------------------------------------------------ 10. nations

msg.flags.intro = Exercise ten. Nations. Each nation flies its own flag and writes in its own script. You have been given the flags and the languages maps. Find a flag or a sign, match it to the map, and guess anywhere in the right nation.

obj.flags.map = Show the flags map
obj.flags.languages = Show the languages map
obj.flags.guess = Guess in the right nation

explain.flags = You were in the nation shown in green: anywhere inside its borders would do.
fail.flags = That's another nation. Find a flag or a sign and match it to the map.

# ------------------------------------------------------------------ 11. the roadside

msg.roadside.intro = Exercise eleven. Roadside furniture. Bollards, road markings and utility poles are built differently from region to region. You have been given maps of all three. Inspect the roadside. Yes, really. Then guess somewhere they are built the same way.

obj.roadside.bollards = Show the bollards map
obj.roadside.lines = Show the road lines map
obj.roadside.guess = Guess where the roadside matches

explain.roadside = Places with the same bollards and the same road markings as here would do.
fail.roadside = The bollards or the road markings there are different. Look at the roadside again.

# ------------------------------------------------------------------ 12. architecture

msg.architecture.intro = Exercise twelve. Architecture. Houses are built in styles that change between nations, and within them. You have been given the architecture map. Look at the houses, find their style on the map, and guess.

obj.architecture.map = Show the architecture map
obj.architecture.guess = Guess where houses look like these

explain.architecture = Places where the houses are built in the same style as here would do.
fail.architecture = The houses there are built in another style. Compare them with the map's pictures.

# ------------------------------------------------------------------ 13. landmarks

msg.landmarks.intro = Final exercise. Landmarks. A few famous places exist nowhere else. If you can see one, you know exactly where you are, which makes this the easiest exercise, and the one trainees most often fail. You have been given the landmarks map. Find the landmark, look it up, and guess.

obj.landmarks.map = Show the landmarks map
obj.landmarks.guess = Guess at the landmark

explain.landmarks = That was {landmark}: anywhere near it would do.
fail.landmarks = That's not near {landmark}. Find it on the landmarks map.

msg.finish = Congratulations. You have completed the Xenocorp Training Programme, and are now qualified for deployment. Your certificate has been sent to your next of kin.
